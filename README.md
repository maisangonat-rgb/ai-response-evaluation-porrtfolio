# AI Response Evaluation — Demonstration

Purpose: show a consistent way to compare AI answers for accuracy, relevance, completeness, clarity, and responsible communication. This is a self-created practice sample, not a real employer assessment.

## Sample user question
“Can I use Excel for accounting, or do I need QuickBooks?”

### Response A
“Excel can be used for basic bookkeeping, such as tracking income and expenses. QuickBooks is accounting software designed for tasks such as invoicing, bank reconciliation, and financial reporting. The best choice depends on the business's complexity, controls, and reporting needs.”

### Response B
“Excel is not accounting software, so it cannot be used for accounting. Every business must use QuickBooks, and it automatically guarantees accurate financial statements.”

## Ratings (1–5)
| Criterion | A | B | Rationale |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Accuracy | 5 | 1 | B makes absolute and misleading claims. |
| Relevance | 5 | 4 | Both address the question, but B's advice is unreliable. |
| Completeness | 4 | 2 | A gives useful differences but could mention business size and compliance needs. |
| Clarity | 5 | 4 | Both are clear, but B's certainty is misleading. |
| Responsibility | 5 | 1 | A avoids guarantees; B wrongly promises automatic accuracy. |

**Judgement:** Response A is stronger because it is balanced and avoids unsupported guarantees.

**Example rationale:** “I prefer Response A because it distinguishes a flexible spreadsheet from dedicated accounting software without claiming one tool is mandatory for every business. Response B makes absolute claims and incorrectly guarantees accurate financial statements.”

## Quality checklist
- Separate facts from opinions.
- Flag absolute claims such as “always” and “guarantees.”
- Do not invent facts.
- Follow the task's rubric.
- Explain scores with evidence.
- State what needs verification when uncertain.
